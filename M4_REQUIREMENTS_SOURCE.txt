Dear Interns,

Milestone 4 is not a completely new module. This is the final integration and presentation layer of everything we have built in Milestones 1, 2 and 3. Your goal is to convert the individual modules into one professional, working cybersecurity platform. By the end of M4, an analyst should be able to open the dashboard, understand the current security posture, identify the most critical threats, investigate an incident, view its attack chain and threat intelligence, understand why the incident received its risk score, and see recommended actions. Focus on making the complete flow work end-to-end rather than adding unnecessary complexity.

Objective
Develop, integrate, test, and finalize an interactive AI-assisted cybersecurity monitoring dashboard that provides real-time visibility into security events, detected threats, risk scores, attack chains, vulnerabilities, threat intelligence, and recommended response actions.

18. TASK 14 — AI-Assisted Risk Explanation
This can be the eye-catching feature.

Instead of displaying:

Risk Score = 94
show:

Why is this threat Critical?
The incident received a critical risk score because:

• The affected asset is classified as Critical.
• The ML model detected highly anomalous behavior.
• A malicious IOC was identified.
• The associated vulnerability has a high CVSS score.
• Multiple related security events indicate a possible attack chain.
This makes the system explainable, rather than just producing numbers.
